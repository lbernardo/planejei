# Planejei — scope

**English** · [Português (Brasil)](SCOPE-pt-br.md)

Planejei is software for designing **built-in furniture and custom cabinetry**. Its workflow is
inspired by Promob. It is a sibling of [GeoArq](../../sketchuo) and shares its engine (Rust +
wgpu + egui), its navigation and its camera shortcuts. GeoArq draws the room; Planejei furnishes
it, details it and **prices** it.

## Who it is for

- **Cabinet makers and custom-furniture shops**: they need a reliable cutting plan, parts list
  and quote, fast, to close the sale and send the job to production.
- **Architects and interior designers**: they want to specify the cabinetry with real
  measurements and show it to the client.

## How it differs from Promob

| Promob | Planejei |
|---|---|
| Expensive, licensed per module (render, cutting, quoting) | Everything included |
| Mostly Windows-only and heavy | Native and lightweight, on Mac **and** Windows |
| Tied to the manufacturer's catalogue | Its own parametric library, with editable prices and sheets |
| The room is drawn inside it | A simple built-in room **or** one imported from GeoArq (`.geoarq`) |

## Data model

- **Room**: rectangular walls (width × depth × ceiling height × thickness), or a mesh imported
  from a `.geoarq` file (visual reference only).
- **Parametric module**:
  - type, W × H × D, position (x, y, z), rotation (0/90/180/270°);
  - number of doors, drawers and shelves, toe-kick height;
  - carcass, front and back materials.
- **Board** (generated by the module):
  - name, length × width × thickness, material;
  - edge banding on each of its 4 sides, and whether it follows the wood grain;
  - the 3D box it occupies.
- **Hardware**: hinges, drawer slides, handles, adjustable feet, shelf supports.
- **Catalogue**:
  - sheets (material, thickness, size, price);
  - edge banding (R$/m) and hardware (R$/unit);
  - labour and margin (%).

### Construction rules (industry standard, editable)

- Full-height sides. Bottom and top fit between the sides. A 6 mm back sits in a groove.
- Base units have a recessed toe kick; wall units have none.
- Doors and drawer fronts are full overlay, with a 2 mm gap on each side.
- Hinges: 2 for doors up to 90 cm tall, 3 up to 160 cm, 4 above that.
- Drawers:
  - each one is a front, 2 sides, a back and a bottom;
  - full-extension slides, as long as the usable depth rounded down to a multiple of 5 cm.
- Edge banding on visible edges: all 4 sides of fronts, only the front edge of carcass parts.

## Cutting plan

- Groups boards by material and thickness.
- Standard sheet is 2750 × 1850 mm, with a 10 mm trim and a 4 mm saw kerf (all editable).
- Guillotine algorithm:
  - *best area fit*, largest boards first;
  - it rotates a board when the material has no grain.
- Draws each sheet with its yield (%) and exports **SVG** and **CSV**.

## Quote

- Sheets (whole, or per m² plus waste), edge banding (m), hardware (units), labour (%) and
  margin (%).
- Exports an **HTML** report ready to print or save as PDF.

## Phases

1. **MVP** ✅ (this version)
   - Rectangular room, `.geoarq` import as a reference
   - Modules: door base unit, drawer base unit, wall unit, tall/pantry unit, wardrobe,
     open shelf unit
   - Insertion snapped to the wall and next to the last module; move, rotate, duplicate, delete
   - Editable properties, parts list, cutting plan, quote, save/open `.planejei`
2. **Production**:
   - part labels (with a code);
   - export to CNC machines and optimizers: DXF per part, CSV in the Corte Certo/Optimizer
     format;
   - hinge and cam-lock drilling;
   - reusable sheet offcuts.
3. **Design**: countertops (granite/MDF), corner units (L and U), free-form (non-rectangular)
   walls, sliding and glass doors, profile handles, lighting.
4. **Presentation**: wood textures (Duratex/Guararapes/Arauco patterns), realistic rendering,
   and dimensioned views and elevations for the client folder.
5. **Store**: manufacturer catalogues, clients, proposals and orders.
